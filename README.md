## Where I Work

### [HGMServers](https://hgmserve.rs) — Owner & Creator
> Modded Call of Duty servers, plus a Minecraft server, across Europe and North America since 2022.

- Vanilla-focused multiplayer and Zombies with real-time leaderboards, weekly challenges and credits
- The hosts, the Java site and API, the Discord bots and the monitoring
- IW4MAdmin plugin development
- Public API ([docs](https://hgmserve.rs/api/docs))

### [gameserve.rs](https://gameserve.rs) — Owner & Creator
> Every modded Call of Duty server in one list.

- Plutonium, IW4x, AlterWare, Aurora, HorizonMW, CoD4x & S2x side by side
- 20+ titles: COD1-4, MW2/3, WaW, BO1-3, Ghosts, AW, IW, MWR, H2M and WWII
- Refreshed every 20 seconds, with history and trends behind every number
- Free public API, no key needed ([docs](https://gameserve.rs/docs))
- Next.js, React, TypeScript & PostgreSQL

### [F1Dash](https://f1dash.net) — Owner & Creator
> A timing console for Formula 1 race weekends.

- Live timing, a track map with every car on it, weather, team radio and full session replay
- Laid out like the screens on a pit wall rather than a fan site
- **[GridDash](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/griddash-race-companion/id6773511792)**, the F1Dash race companion for iPhone: live timing, track map, telemetry, standings and the calendar. Free, no in-app purchases
